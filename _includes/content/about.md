# **About**

NUOMI Lab is an artificial intelligence (AI) research group at London South Bank University (LSBU). Our name is short for **Normative and User-Oriented Machine Intelligence**: we study what AI systems *should* do, not only what they *can* do; we build technology around the people and communities who use it and who are affected by it.

**Normative.** Every AI system encodes choices about whose goals count, whose values drive outcomes, and who bears the risks of AI system failures. Our research makes the values implied by the technical design of AI systems explicit choices; we ground our choices in human values and social context, and develop machine learning (ML) methods that hold AI systems accountable to these values.

**User-Oriented.** An AI system is only useful if the people relying on it can understand it and decide when and how to use it, and if the system complements human expertise and capabilities. We design AI systems personalised to the needs of diverse real users making decisions in specific real-life settings.

In our work, we collaborate closely with communities (mental health advocacy groups, clinicians, educators), AI research groups worldwide (across academia and industry), as well as policy makers interested in governing the impact of frontier AI technologies.
